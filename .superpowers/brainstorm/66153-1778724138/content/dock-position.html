Where should the ramp range button live in the dock?

The button shows the lowest + highest ramp colors and opens the control panel when clicked. Pick the position that feels most natural.

🎨
☀️
Option A

Between launcher and ramp

Ramp range button sits left of the color swatches, adjacent to the palette launcher. Groups the two "entry" controls together on the left.

🎨
☀️
Option B

Between ramp and theme toggle

Ramp range button caps the right side of the color swatches. The ramp "bookends" itself — endpoint summary immediately follows the full sequence.

🎨
☀️
Option C

Far right (after theme toggle)

Ramp range button is the rightmost item. Theme toggle and range summary become a utility cluster on the far end, separate from the color swatches.
